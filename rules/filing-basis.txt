[ca_saas_vendor_tax]
This is a DRAFT vendor letter for the synthetic demo. It is not sent.

software as not subject to sales and use tax for the signed CA-RAS-001
window. A customer generally cannot claim a refund from CDTFA
(McClain v. Sav-On Drugs). This note is demo research text, not verified
filing advice.
